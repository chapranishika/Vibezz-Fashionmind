Privacy Policy

Vibezz FashionMind · last updated 31 August 2026

Vibezz FashionMind (“the app”) is a personal portfolio project that shows a feed of currently rising fashion search terms and helps a visitor shop those trends across third-party retailers. This page explains what data the app handles.

1. Pinterest data

The app uses the Pinterest API in read-only mode to retrieve trending keyword data only. It authenticates with the developer’s own Pinterest account via OAuth. The app does not read, create, edit, or delete Pins or Boards, does not access any other Pinterest user’s account, and does not store or resell any Pinterest data. Trending keywords are displayed to the visitor and cached on the server for a few hours purely to reduce the number of API calls.

2. Account data you provide

If you create a FashionMind account, we store your email address, a generated customer identifier, and the contents of your cart and wishlist. This is kept in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database and is used only to operate your session. You can request deletion at any time (see Contact).

3. Shopping links

Product links open third-party retailer websites (for example Myntra, Ajio, Nykaa Fashion, H&M, Amazon.in). Those sites have their own privacy policies; we are not responsible for their content or data practices. Prices shown in the app may be estimates and should be confirmed on the retailer’s site.

4. Third-party services

  • Pinterest API — trending keyword data (read-only).
  • SerpApi / Google Shopping — live product listings for a search query.
  • Google Gemini — powers the optional AI stylist chat; your message text is sent to Google’s API to generate a reply.
  • Supabase — database and authentication.
  • Vercel / Hugging Face Spaces — hosting.

5. Cookies & local storage

The app stores a login token and your cart locally in your browser (localStorage). No third-party advertising or tracking cookies are set by the app.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except the service providers listed in section 4 as needed to run the app.
